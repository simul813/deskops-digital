No. It is a freelance ops tracker for clients, invoices, payments, expenses, and a Friday dashboard—not accounting software.

No. The tax field is a planning placeholder only—something that helps you remember to set money aside for later review with a qualified professional. It is not a calculation of what you owe, and this site does not give tax advice.
